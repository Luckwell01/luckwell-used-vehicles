About Luckwell

Luckwell Co., Ltd. was founded in 1996 and has over 20 years of experience specializing in the used motorcycle and car trading industry. Headquartered in Taiwan, we are committed to providing reliable quality at competitive prices — client satisfaction is always our top priority.

Luckwell Co., Ltd. operates one core business unit: the Used Motorcycle & Car BU. In our used motorcycle and car division, we source and trade high-quality pre-owned motorcycles and cars, offering buyers dependable transportation solutions at affordable prices.

Every vehicle undergoes a thorough pre-shipment inspection to ensure safety, reliability, and performance standards are met before delivery — giving our clients complete peace of mind with every purchase.

  • Used Motorcycle Trading (domestic & export)
  • Used Car Trading (domestic & export)
  • Pre-shipment inspection for every vehicle
  • Vehicle sourcing & inspection services
  • Bulk procurement for dealers and fleet buyers
